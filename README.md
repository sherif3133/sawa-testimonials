# Sawa Testimonials

A wall of student testimonials plus a review form where students send written, voice or video reviews.

Shortcodes: `[sawa_testimonials]` and `[sawa_review_form]`. `[sawa_gift_form]` is the old gift page and now shows the review form.

The review form asks for the student's Skool name and email. In **Testimonials → Review queue**, **Approve** puts the review on the wall and gives the student Sawa App access (an approved Sawa Tracker account and a welcome email with a set-password link). **Reject** sends an email asking for a better review (default text in Settings) and moves the review to the trash.
