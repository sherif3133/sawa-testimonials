# Sawa Testimonials

Review form (with the Sawa App gift), review queue and testimonial wall. The shared notes for all Sawa apps are in `../CLAUDE.md`.

- Approving a review (Review queue) creates an approved `../sawa-tracker` account for the student and emails them; rejecting emails them to write a better review.
- Bump `Version:` and `SAWA_T_VER` in `sawa-testimonials.php` for every release.
